Heds Chäs?

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

Heds Chäs? is an app for tracking, rating and sharing cheese with friends. It is developed by Maurin Dai in Switzerland, who is responsible for the processing of your data under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

In short: what you post in Heds Chäs? (your profile and check-ins) is stored in Apple's iCloud so your friends can see it. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

Your account

Heds Chäs? has no password or email sign-up. It uses your iCloud account: Apple gives the app an anonymous identifier for your iCloud account, which lets it recognise your profile. The app never sees your Apple Account email address or name unless you type your name into your profile.

What is stored, and who can see it

The following is stored in the app's database on Apple's iCloud (CloudKit public database):

  • Profile: username, display name, and optionally a short bio, a profile photo, your home country or canton, and whether your profile is private.
  • Check-ins: which cheese you tried, your rating, flavour tags, where you bought it, an optional note and an optional photo, and the time.
  • Follows: whom you follow, who follows you, and follow requests with their answers.
  • Catalog contributions: cheeses and producers (e.g. Käsereien) you add to the shared catalog.

This information is visible to other people using Heds Chäs?. If you make your profile private, the app only shows your check-ins to people whose follow request you accepted. Please note that this protection is enforced by the app: the data itself is stored in the same shared iCloud database as everyone else's, so do not post anything in a check-in you would not want other users to see.

Photos

The app only accesses photos you actively choose or take with the camera. Before a photo is uploaded it is reduced in size and its metadata, including any location, is removed. The app does not read the rest of your photo library.

Information that stays private to you

Your favourite producers and your wishlist are stored in your own iCloud key-value storage. Only you can see them; they sync between your own devices.

Where your data is stored

All of the above is stored and processed by Apple as part of iCloud/CloudKit, under Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple may store data on servers outside Switzerland, including in the EU and the United States. The developer does not run any other servers and does not receive copies of your data.

Why we process it

Only to provide the app's features: showing your check-ins, letting friends follow you, and building a shared cheese catalog. Under the GDPR the legal basis is performance of the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)).

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, usage tracking or cross-app tracking.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No access to your contacts, email address or phone number.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete individual check-ins at any time. In Settings → Delete account you can delete your profile, photo, check-ins, follows and requests, and your private lists, immediately. Cheeses and producers you added to the shared catalog remain available to others but are no longer linked to a profile.

Your rights

You can access and correct your data directly in the app. You also have the right to request information about, correction or deletion of your data, to object to processing, and to receive a copy of your data. To do so, contact us below. You may also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, in the EU, to your local data protection authority.

Children

Heds Chäs? is not directed to children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
